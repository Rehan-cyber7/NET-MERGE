Plexo Mobile is a mobile port of [Plexo](https://github.com/anmolkapil/plexo) by Anmol Kapil (MIT, see LICENSE).
It reuses Plexo's design tokens (colors, Manrope and Roboto Mono fonts in `src/assets`), its app icon and its
chunked multi-network download design. The mobile engines and UI code are new.

"Plexo" and its icon are the original author's. Before publishing to the App Store or Google Play,
ask the author or rename and re-icon the app.
